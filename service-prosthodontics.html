Overview

What is prosthodontics?

Prosthodontics replaces or restores teeth that are missing, heavily worn or structurally weakened. Restorations are made to match your bite and appearance, and are planned around the health of the remaining teeth and gums.

Options range from a single crown to a bridge, or a removable or implant-supported denture. Your dentist will discuss the advantages and limitations of each, including what it means for cleaning and maintenance.

What we treat

  • A tooth weakened by a large filling, root canal treatment or a crack
  • One or more missing teeth
  • Heavily worn or eroded teeth
  • Ill-fitting or broken dentures
  • Teeth that need a fixed bridge
  • Full-mouth rehabilitation in selected cases

What to expect

  1. Assessment, photographs and impressions or scans
  2. A treatment plan covering options, materials and cost
  3. Trial fitting where needed
  4. Fitting of the final restoration
  5. Adjustments and a review appointment

At a glance

FAQs

Prosthodontics questions

How long do crowns and bridges last?

Often ten years or more with good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, although this varies from person to person.

Will it look natural?

Restorations are matched to the colour and shape of your teeth. The final appearance is discussed with you before treatment begins.

Can I eat normally?

Yes, once fitted. Very hard foods should still be avoided because they can damage any restoration.

How do I clean a bridge?

Use floss or interdental brushes under the false tooth as shown by your dentist, along with normal brushing.

Do dentures need replacing?

Dentures wear and the mouth changes over time, so they may need relining or replacement after several years.

Is a temporary crown needed?

Often yes, while the final restoration is being made. It protects the tooth and maintains appearance in the meantime.
